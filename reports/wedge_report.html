The ledger bridge

The biggest lift is the worst action on the board

Free shipping is the only lever with a statistically confident, positive selection effect. It is also one of the two rejected actions — because it is a global-channel lever, and the agent channel mediates only a sliver of total demand.

the naive read  →  +1.69pp selection lift (+34% relative)  →  looks like a clear win

Even inside the agent channel, shipping-subsidized orders that would have arrived anyway (cannibalization) outweigh the volume and margin gain: agent-channel contribution alone is −$10,118. Then the same discount applies to all ~9,400 non-agent orders/year, at −$178,600 — the number a selection-lift metric never sees.

This is the correction this project's economics engine exists to make: Δtotal = agent_gain − non_agent_exposure, not lift per agent opportunity. The lift is real. The recommendation built from the lift alone would have been wrong.

Breakeven channel share

Neither rejected action has a breakeven

The usual next question is "at what agent-channel share would this pay off?" For both global-scope actions here, the honest answer is: none. Agent-channel contribution is linear in agent volume and passes through the origin — since it's already negative at current volume, scaling the agent channel up only scales the loss, it never crosses zero.

No breakeven
Free standard shipping — agent-channel contribution alone is already −$10,118 at current volume (cannibalization exceeds the volume/margin gain). No finite increase in agent-mediated demand fixes that; the per-order economics are wrong, not the scale.
No breakeven
Cut list price 5% — agent-channel contribution alone is −$32,952: the price cut produced zero measured selection change, so the discount is pure margin loss with no offsetting volume, in either channel.
